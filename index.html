      <h2>03 — LAB</h2>

      <h3>Laboratory</h3>

      <p>A research and exploration division investigating material properties, spatial conditions, and form generation.</p>


      <div class="project">

        <div class="project-title">BIG SLURP</div>

        <div class="project-status">Ongoing investigation · Proposed installation</div>

        <p>BIG SLURP explores continuous inversion as a spatial condition. A textile body of constant length is turned through itself by a distributed system, producing a persistent loop without origin or resolution. The project treats inversion not as an event, but as a sustained state in which inside and outside remain temporary and reversible.</p>

      </div>


      <div class="project">

        <div class="project-title">Anodized Aluminum Surface Study</div>

        <div class="project-status">Material research · Ongoing</div>

        <p>Investigation of microscopic photographs of anodized aluminum surfaces, revealing a hidden chromatic landscape. What appears macroscopically as uniform gray resolves at the microscale into complex patterns of colorful crystalline structures. This research examines the gap between perceived and actual material properties, questioning assumptions about surface and color at different scales of observation.</p>

      </div>

    </div>

  </section>
