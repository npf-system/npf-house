03 — LAB

Laboratory

A research and exploration division investigating material properties, spatial conditions, and form generation.

BIG SLURP
Ongoing investigation · Proposed installation

BIG SLURP explores continuous inversion as a spatial condition. A textile body of constant length is turned through itself by a distributed system, producing a persistent loop without origin or resolution. The project treats inversion not as an event, but as a sustained state in which inside and outside remain temporary and reversible.

Anodized Aluminum Surface Study
Material research · Ongoing

Investigation of microscopic photographs of anodized aluminum surfaces, revealing a hidden chromatic landscape. What appears macroscopically as uniform gray resolves at the microscale into complex patterns of colorful crystalline structures. This research examines the gap between perceived and actual material properties, questioning assumptions about surface and color at different scales of observation.
